## Why

The detail has room for the whole logo; cropping it to a square, as the card
must, cuts away lettering on a wide logo (owner, 2026-10-11).

## What Changes

- On the detail's cover the logo plate takes the logo's proportions at a fixed
  height and shows the whole logo; a monogram or short code keeps the square.
- On a phone the cover stacks: plate, then the status block beneath it.
- The list's card keeps its square, cropped plate.
