## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A tournament states who keeps its registrations
Each tournament SHALL carry a stored value naming who keeps its list of entrants, with exactly two members: **Squire keeps them**, meaning fencers register in the application and Squire manages what follows, and **the organizer keeps them**, meaning the list is maintained outside Squire and reaches it by import.

The value SHALL be stored with the tournament and SHALL be a property of the tournament rather than of the account reading it: every member of a tournament's console team SHALL see the same value. It SHALL NOT be derived or re-derived from the tournament's contents at any point. A tournament with imported rows and no in-app registrations SHALL NOT become organizer-kept on that evidence, and one that has been registered for in the application SHALL NOT become Squire-kept on that evidence; the value records what the organizer decided.

A tournament created after this capability exists SHALL be Squire-kept unless the organizer chooses otherwise. Every tournament that existed before it SHALL be Squire-kept, because that is what all of them were.

This value SHALL be independent of the four features fixed by `tournament-modes`. It SHALL NOT be one of them, SHALL NOT be counted when easy and advanced mode are named, and SHALL NOT be read as implying any of them: an organizer-kept tournament MAY have any combination of the four, including none.

#### Scenario: Stored with the tournament
- **WHEN** two organizers on one tournament's console team open its Setup phase
- **THEN** both see the same value for who keeps its registrations

#### Scenario: Not derived from contents
- **WHEN** an organizer imports a table of fifty entrants into a Squire-kept tournament
- **THEN** the tournament stays Squire-kept and the rows are stored

#### Scenario: Existing tournaments are Squire's
- **WHEN** a tournament that existed before this capability is read
- **THEN** it is Squire-kept, whatever it holds

#### Scenario: Independent of the features
- **WHEN** an organizer sets a tournament to organizer-kept
- **THEN** none of the four features changes, and the tournament is still named easy or advanced by those four alone

### Requirement: Squire runs nothing against a tournament it does not keep
WHEN a tournament is organizer-kept, Squire SHALL run no part of its registration lifecycle against it. The exclusion SHALL be made once, over tournaments, rather than as a condition repeated in each lifecycle pass, so that no pass added later can omit it and no registration created by any path can be reached by one.

Specifically, such a tournament SHALL:

- receive no reminder, expiry notice, payment instruction, surcharge notice or confirmation mail generated by the lifecycle;
- have no reservation expired for non-payment;
- have no registration demoted to the substitute queue, whether the seating deadline passes or the organizer settles by hand;
- have no bank feed polled and no transaction reconciled on a schedule.

The exclusion SHALL hold whatever the tournament's payment settings say. An organizer-kept tournament that carries a payment mode, a payment window and a bank account SHALL still have nothing run against it; those settings are retained, as every hidden setting is, and are simply never acted upon.

#### Scenario: The lifecycle passes it by
- **WHEN** the lifecycle runs against an organizer-kept tournament holding registrations older than any configured window
- **THEN** nothing expires, nothing is demoted, no mail is sent, and no bank feed is polled

#### Scenario: A registration created by any path is safe
- **WHEN** a registration is created on an organizer-kept tournament by a path that does not mark it dormant
- **THEN** the lifecycle still does not reach it, because the tournament was never selected

#### Scenario: Payment settings do not revive it
- **WHEN** an organizer-kept tournament carries a bank account, an immediate payment mode and a five-day window
- **THEN** the values are retained and no window opens, no reminder is sent and nothing expires

### Requirement: Organizer-kept is a cause of registration dormancy
Being organizer-kept SHALL be a cause in the dormancy predicate fixed by `registration`'s **One dormancy predicate governs the lifecycle passes**, alongside the payments feature being off and a registration having been issued from an imported row.

This SHALL hold notwithstanding the tournament-level exclusion above, because the predicate governs paths the scheduler's selection does not reach: the count of pending demotions the console states, and the settlement an organizer triggers by hand. A tournament the organizer keeps SHALL demote nobody when its organizer presses settle, for the same reason the scheduler demotes nobody on it.

The per-registration mark that makes an issued registration dormant by origin SHALL remain in force and SHALL NOT be removed as redundant. It covers what the tournament-level exclusion does not: registrations issued from imported rows on a tournament Squire otherwise keeps.

#### Scenario: Manual settlement demotes nobody
- **WHEN** the organizer of an organizer-kept tournament settles seating by hand
- **THEN** no registration is demoted, every seat is kept, and the tournament is recorded as settled

#### Scenario: The console states no pending demotions
- **WHEN** the organizer of an organizer-kept tournament opens the settle action
- **THEN** the count of registrations that would be demoted is zero

#### Scenario: The per-row mark still guards a mixed tournament
- **WHEN** rows are imported and issued into a Squire-kept tournament and the lifecycle runs
- **THEN** the issued registrations receive nothing, exactly as they do today

### Requirement: In-app registration never opens on an organizer-kept tournament
WHEN a tournament is organizer-kept, the system SHALL refuse every in-app registration submission, and SHALL refuse it with a reason distinct from the registration window being shut. Nothing was ever open and nothing is late; the registration is kept elsewhere, and a fencer SHALL NOT be told they missed a deadline that never existed.

The refusal SHALL be decided in the same gate that decides every other reason a submission may not proceed, and SHALL be evaluated before them, since it is not that this tournament's window is closed but that it has no window here.

An amendment to an existing registration SHALL be refused for the same reason, since amendment availability follows registration availability.

#### Scenario: Registration refused with its own reason
- **WHEN** a fencer submits a registration for an organizer-kept, published tournament inside its registration window
- **THEN** the submission is refused with the organizer-kept reason, not with the closed reason

#### Scenario: Refused before every other reason
- **WHEN** a fencer submits a registration for an organizer-kept tournament that is also unpublished and past its closing date
- **THEN** the refusal states that the organizer keeps the registrations

#### Scenario: Amendment refused alike
- **WHEN** a fencer holding a registration on a tournament that has since become organizer-kept attempts to amend it
- **THEN** the amendment is refused with the same reason

### Requirement: The choice is made at creation and changed in Setup
The organizer SHALL choose who keeps a tournament's registrations when the tournament is created, with Squire-kept preselected, and SHALL be able to change it afterwards on the Setup phase's `OTHER` tab.

The choice SHALL be presented in a section of its own, beside the tournament mode rather than inside it. It SHALL NOT be offered as a fifth feature in the Tournament Mode dialog, because that dialog's own explanation is that turning a feature off hides settings without changing behaviour, and this choice does the opposite.

The section SHALL state the tournament's current answer in words and SHALL carry help text saying what the organizer-kept answer means in consequence — that fencers cannot register in the application and that Squire sends them nothing — rather than naming the two ends alone.

#### Scenario: Chosen at creation
- **WHEN** an organizer creates a tournament and selects that the organizer keeps its registrations
- **THEN** the tournament is created organizer-kept and the console opens on Setup

#### Scenario: Default at creation
- **WHEN** an organizer creates a tournament and does not touch the choice
- **THEN** the tournament is Squire-kept

#### Scenario: Stated and changed on OTHER
- **WHEN** the organizer opens `OTHER` on an organizer-kept tournament
- **THEN** the section states that the organizer keeps its registrations and offers a control to change it

#### Scenario: Not a feature in the mode dialog
- **WHEN** the organizer opens the Tournament Mode dialog
- **THEN** it offers the four features and no fifth, and says nothing about who keeps the registrations

### Requirement: Changing who keeps the registrations is confirmed and states its effect
Changing the value on a tournament SHALL require confirmation, and the confirmation SHALL state what the change does rather than that a setting is changing. Both directions SHALL be confirmed, because both have consequences.

Changing to organizer-kept SHALL state that in-app registration will close and that Squire will stop sending mail and stop expiring reservations. Where the tournament already holds in-app registrations, the confirmation SHALL state how many, because those are the fencers whose registration Squire will stop managing.

Changing to Squire-kept SHALL state that in-app registration will open according to the tournament's registration window and that the lifecycle will begin running against the tournament's registrations.

Declining SHALL change nothing. Confirming SHALL apply the change immediately, as the Tournament Mode dialog does, without a save control.

No registration SHALL be deleted, expired, cancelled or demoted by the change itself, in either direction. What the change alters is what Squire will do next, never what has already happened.

#### Scenario: Switching to organizer-kept counts what is affected
- **WHEN** the organizer of a tournament holding twelve in-app registrations switches it to organizer-kept
- **THEN** the confirmation states that registration will close, that Squire will send nothing further, and that twelve registrations are already held

#### Scenario: Declining changes nothing
- **WHEN** the organizer declines the confirmation
- **THEN** the value is unchanged and nothing is written

#### Scenario: The change writes no registration
- **WHEN** a tournament with reserved, paid and expired registrations is switched in either direction
- **THEN** every registration keeps its state, its placements, its variable symbol and its credited amount
