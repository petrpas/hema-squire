## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Discipline classification
A discipline SHALL carry its classification as separate fields: **weapon**, **gender**, and **material**. Gender SHALL be one of Open, Women, or Men, and material SHALL be one of Steel or Plastic; both are closed sets. Weapon SHALL offer the HEMA taxonomy weapons — LS Longsword, SA Sabre, RA Single Rapier, RD Rapier & Dagger, SB Sword & Buckler, SS Single Sidesword, ST Singlestick, KN Knife, ME Messer, SM Smallsword, SD Sidesword & Dagger — and SHALL additionally accept a weapon the taxonomy does not name, so that an organizer can declare a discipline of their own. A discipline whose weapon is outside the taxonomy SHALL require an explicit name, since none can be generated for it; a discipline whose weapon is within the taxonomy SHALL have its name generated by default and SHALL allow it to be replaced. A generated name SHALL state that a discipline is a team discipline, so that a tournament offering both an individual and a team discipline classified alike does not present a fencer with two entries bearing the same name.

A discipline stored before a weapon joined the taxonomy keeps the weapon text it was stored with: a discipline whose weapon was typed as `Messer` remains a discipline outside the taxonomy and is not rewritten to `ME`.

#### Scenario: Classification entered as fields
- **WHEN** the organizer adds a discipline choosing sabre, women, and plastic
- **THEN** the discipline records those three values separately, and its name defaults to the taxonomy name for that combination

#### Scenario: Team discipline name states its kind
- **WHEN** the organizer adds an individual longsword discipline and a team longsword discipline, accepting the generated name for both
- **THEN** the two names differ, and the team discipline's name states that it is a team discipline

#### Scenario: Side weapon offered by the taxonomy
- **WHEN** the organizer adds a discipline choosing Messer from the weapon list, open, and steel
- **THEN** the discipline is created with weapon `ME`, its name is generated without the organizer entering one, and its slug is generated as `ME`

#### Scenario: Weapon outside the taxonomy
- **WHEN** the organizer adds a discipline whose weapon is `Tešák` typed as their own
- **THEN** it is accepted, and the organizer is required to give it a name

#### Scenario: Custom weapon without a name
- **WHEN** the organizer adds a discipline with a weapon outside the taxonomy and leaves the name empty
- **THEN** the discipline is refused with a message stating that a name is required for this weapon

#### Scenario: Stored custom weapon not rewritten
- **WHEN** a tournament holds a discipline stored with the typed weapon `Messer` before Messer joined the taxonomy
- **THEN** that discipline keeps weapon `Messer`, its name and slug are unchanged, and it carries no HEMA Ratings counterpart

### Requirement: Taxonomy code derived for external joins
A discipline's **taxonomy code** SHALL be derived from its weapon, gender, and material, in the form the HEMA taxonomy uses: an optional material prefix, the weapon code, and an optional gender suffix (`LS`, `SAW`, `Plastic LSM`). Every taxonomy weapon code SHALL be exactly two characters, and the gender, when stated, SHALL be the single character after it. A taxonomy code SHALL therefore be read back by position rather than by its last character, so that a weapon code ending in a letter that also names a gender (`SM` Smallsword) is not mistaken for a gender. The code SHALL NOT be stored as a discipline's own field and SHALL NOT identify a discipline. It SHALL be the key by which the system joins a discipline to anything outside itself, so that disciplines sharing a classification share that join and cannot drift apart. A discipline whose weapon is outside the taxonomy SHALL derive a taxonomy code that no external mapping recognizes, and SHALL be treated as having no external counterpart rather than as an error.

#### Scenario: Tiers share one external join
- **WHEN** a tournament offers two longsword disciplines and the organizer configures the external mapping for longsword
- **THEN** one mapping governs both disciplines, and there is no way to configure them differently

#### Scenario: Custom weapon has no counterpart
- **WHEN** a discipline's weapon is outside the taxonomy
- **THEN** it resolves to no external counterpart, and its absence is not reported as a failure

#### Scenario: Weapon code ending in a gender letter
- **WHEN** the codes `SM`, `SMW` and `Plastic SMM` are read back into a classification
- **THEN** they read as smallsword open steel, smallsword women steel, and smallsword men plastic

#### Scenario: Every generated code reads back to itself
- **WHEN** any taxonomy code the system generates is read back into a weapon, gender and material and derived again
- **THEN** the derived code equals the one read
