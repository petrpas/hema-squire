## Why

A discipline's `code` is its identity, its classification, and its HEMA Ratings join key
all at once. One string, three jobs, and each job wants a different cardinality:
identity wants as many rows as the organizer invents, classification wants one label per
row freely repeated, and the HR join wants one fetch per distinct weapon × gender ×
material. Collapsing them forces all three to be equal, and `UNIQUE(tournament_id, code)`
makes that collapse binding.

Two ordinary tournament shapes are therefore inexpressible today:

- **Tiers.** A tournament that splits longsword into a top-fencer bracket and an open
  bracket cannot do it. Both are longsword; the second add is rejected with
  `discipline_exists`.
- **Individual and team in the same weapon.** `add-team-disciplines` gave a discipline a
  kind, but not a second identity. A tournament running both individual longsword and
  team longsword cannot express it — the kind is per-code and the code is per-tournament,
  so the two shapes are mutually exclusive within a weapon. The team feature shipped with
  this hole in it.

The taxonomy is also closed: `add_discipline` rejects anything outside the 30 generated
codes, so Messer, Ringen, and mixed-weapon events have nowhere to go.

## What Changes

**A discipline's identity separates from what it is**

- A discipline SHALL be identified within its tournament by a **slug** — short, stable,
  organizer-visible, machine-usable. The slug replaces `code` as the unique key, the URL
  path segment, and the token in exports, sheets, and import parses.
- The slug SHALL be generated by the system from the discipline's classification
  (`LS`, then `LS-2`, `LS-3` on collision), and MAY be overridden by the organizer.
- The slug SHALL be editable while the discipline is unreferenced and frozen once any
  registration or team references it — the same freeze rule `kind` already uses.
- A tournament MAY carry any number of disciplines sharing a classification. Tiers,
  and individual-plus-team in one weapon, become ordinary configurations.

**Classification becomes structured fields**

- A discipline SHALL carry **weapon**, **gender**, and **material** as separate fields
  rather than a packed code. Gender and material stay closed sets (Open/Women/Men;
  Steel/Plastic).
- **Weapon opens up.** The five taxonomy weapons remain offered as suggestions, but a
  discipline MAY name any weapon. A discipline whose weapon is outside the taxonomy has
  no HR rating category and is skipped by the ratings snapshot — an existing null path,
  not a new branch.
- A discipline with a custom weapon SHALL require an explicit name; the taxonomy can no
  longer generate one.

**The HR join keys on classification, not identity**

- The per-tournament HR category map SHALL key on the **taxonomy code** derived from
  weapon × gender × material, not on a discipline's identity. Two tiers of longsword
  share one category and one override, and cannot drift apart.
- The ratings snapshot SHALL fetch once per distinct taxonomy code rather than once per
  discipline row.

**Fencers stop seeing the slug**

- Every fencer-facing presentation of a discipline SHALL show its name alone. The slug is
  an organizer and machine token: console tables, exports, sheets, and API paths.
- With tiers in play, the name is the only thing that distinguishes two disciplines, so
  it carries the whole distinction wherever a fencer chooses or reviews an entry.

**The import parser picks a discipline instead of describing one**

- **BREAKING (internal).** The LLM parse of an imported row SHALL yield a discipline
  **slug chosen from the tournament's offered list**, replacing the weapon/gender/material
  struct it emits today. That struct cannot express a custom weapon and cannot
  distinguish two disciplines sharing a classification, so it stops being a viable output
  type on both counts.
- Where the source row does not identify which of several same-classification disciplines
  is meant — legacy rows never recorded a tier — the parse SHALL record a problem for
  organizer review through the existing decision machinery, rather than guessing.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `discipline-identity`: what identifies a discipline within a tournament, how a slug is
  generated, overridden, and frozen, how classification is structured, and how the
  taxonomy code is derived for external joins.

### Modified Capabilities

- `tournament-admin`: discipline definition gains slug and structured classification,
  loses the code; the Setup discipline table gains a slug field and an open weapon field
- `registration`: disciplines are chosen and presented by name; the slug is the wire
  identifier and is never displayed to a fencer
- `fencer-home`: discipline presentation on the fencer's own pages drops the code
- `data-export`: JSON and Sheets exports carry the slug; the JSON round-trip resolves
  disciplines by slug
- `table-import`: the LLM parse yields a chosen slug rather than a classification struct
- `hr-integration`: the discipline-to-HR-category mapping keys on the taxonomy code, and
  disciplines outside the taxonomy carry no category

## Impact

**Sequencing.** This change stacks on `add-team-disciplines`, which is implemented but
not archived. Its deltas here are written against the post-team wording; the two must be
archived in order.

**Schema.** `disciplines.code` → `slug`, plus new `weapon`, `gender`, `material` columns
backfilled by parsing the old code (30 known values, total coverage).
`UNIQUE(tournament_id, code)` becomes `UNIQUE(tournament_id, slug)` — still correct, no
longer meaning "one longsword per tournament". Every existing row's slug equals its old
code, so stored URLs, exports, and sheet columns keep resolving. No data migration for
`tournaments.hr_category_map` or `hr_snapshot_ratings.discipline_code`: their keys are
already taxonomy codes and stay taxonomy codes.

**Backend.** `models.py` (`Discipline`), `taxonomy.py` (code becomes derived; validation
moves to facets), `routers/tournaments.py` (add/patch/delete by slug, slug generation,
freeze rule), `routers/registrations.py` (`by_code` → `by_slug`), `hr_sync.py`
(fetch per taxonomy code), `importer.py` (`ParsedDiscipline` removed), `sheet.py`,
`sheets_export.py`, `export_json.py`, `emails.py`.

**Frontend.** `api.ts` discipline types, `SetupPanel.tsx` discipline rows (slug and
weapon fields), `TournamentFace.tsx`, `FencerHome.tsx`, `TeamsPanel.tsx` — the fencer
surfaces drop `{code} — {name}` in favour of `{name}`.

**Not in scope.** Tiers carry no eligibility semantics: a tier is a label, and this change
adds no rating cutoff, no seeding, and no automatic assignment of fencers to tiers.
