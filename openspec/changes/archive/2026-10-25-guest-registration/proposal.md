## Why

Entering a tournament today requires a Squire account: Register on a public detail leads to sign-in, and a visitor who does not want an account has no way in. For a one-off visitor, a parent entering children or a club representative entering a group, creating an account is a barrier with nothing behind it. The owner asked for entry without signing up, with the entry then managed through a link in the e-mail.

Allowing it raises a second problem that already exists in a smaller form. Squire holds fencer records without a password: the ones the organizer enrols from a roster, and with this change the guests. `fencer-accounts` promises that such a person can sign up afterwards on the ordinary terms. In practice signup on an address one of these records holds is refused with `409 email_already_registered`; a throwaway test against `backend/app/routers/auth.py` confirmed it on 2026-10-25. Signup does not verify the address, so the obvious fix — let signup take over the record — would hand a stranger every registration and payment filed under an address they typed. The owner decided that creating an account now verifies the address by e-mail, and that only a verified signup takes the record over.

## What Changes

- **Register without signing up.** Selecting Register while signed out still opens sign-in, which now offers both "Create account" and "Register without signing up". Both open the same form: e-mail, name, the optional HEMA Ratings step and language. The guest variant has its own heading and no password field. Filling it in returns the visitor to the tournament's registration form. Selecting the checklist, which is unchanged, and submitting sends the identity and the selection together. A guest registration is a registration in every lifecycle respect: VS, placement, queue, payment window, reminders, expiry, demotion.
- **No account is created for a guest.** The entry is held on a fencer record without credentials. A passwordless record that already holds the address and names the same person is reused. Otherwise the record holds no address and the address is written as the registration's contact address. This is how a parent or a club representative enters several people from one address.
- **An address that belongs to an account is refused** for a guest entry, and the visitor is offered sign-in instead. An address already registered on this tournament is refused as already registered.
- **A manage link.** Every message Squire sends about a guest registration carries a link that opens that registration's management: state, payment instructions and QR, amend, cancel, team rosters. It grants exactly what the account holder's own view grants, and nothing beyond that one registration. After submitting, the browser is taken to that link at once. The key in it changes whenever the registration's recipient address changes, and when the fencer behind it becomes an account. Mail to account holders carries no link. There is no self-service "send me the link again"; a guest who loses the link asks the organizer.
- **BREAKING: account creation verifies the e-mail address.** Signup no longer signs the fencer in. It records a pending signup and mails a verification link. The account exists, and the fencer is signed in, only when the link is followed. Sign-in on an address whose signup is still pending says so and offers to send the link again. Accounts that exist before this change count as verified.
- **A verified signup takes over a passwordless record** on its address, with its registrations, instead of being refused. This removes today's 409 for roster-enrolled fencers as well as for guests.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `guest-registration`: entering a tournament without an account. Covers the guest identity and how it resolves to a fencer record, the manage link (what it grants, which messages carry it, when its key changes), and the guest's management view.

### Modified Capabilities
- `fencer-accounts`: "Account creation with HR binding" verifies the address before the account exists and no longer signs in at submit. A new requirement covers the pending signup, the verification link, resending it and sign-in while pending. "A fencer record may exist without an account": a verified signup on the record's address takes the record over; the two signup scenarios change accordingly. The account form gains its guest variant.
- `registration`: "In-app registration" is open to a guest as well as to an authenticated fencer, on identical terms. "A registration may carry a contact address of its own" gains the guest who enters several people from one address, and it states that the manage link sent to that address belongs to the link, not to the address. Amendment and cancellation through the link are fixed in `guest-registration` and change nothing here.
- `public-browsing`: "An action needing an account leads to sign-in". The sign-in screen raised by Register also offers registering without signing up, and the line stating that registering needs an account goes.
- `routing`: the manage-link URL and the verification-link URL are screens with addresses of their own.

## Impact

- **Backend.** A new `pending_signups` table (address, password hash, name, HR id, club, language, return path, key hash, expiry) and a `manage_key_generation` counter on `Registration`, in one Alembic revision. `routers/auth.py` changes: signup becomes pending, new `verify` and `resend-verification` endpoints, sign-in refuses a pending signup by its own reason, and verification takes over a passwordless record. New `POST /api/tournaments/{slug}/guest-register`, sharing its body with `register`, which is factored into one placement core. The `my-registration` endpoints resolve their registration from either the bearer account or a manage key. `emails.py`: the verification mail, plus the manage link in every send whose fencer holds no password. `substitution.py` bumps the key generation when the recipient address changes.
- **Frontend.** `Login.tsx` gains the "Register without signing up" entry and drops the needs-an-account line. `SignupForm.tsx` gains the guest variant and the "check your mail" outcome. New verification and manage screens, routed in `App.tsx` and built from `routes.ts`. `TournamentDetail.tsx`/`TournamentFace.tsx` submit a held guest identity with the checklist. `api.ts` sends the manage key on the `my-registration` calls. i18n cs/en.
- **Tests.** Backend: guest identity resolution, including the parent with two children and the address of an account; the manage key granting one registration only and dying on rotation; verification and takeover; sign-in while pending. Frontend: the two entries opening one form, the guest form without a password, the manage screen.
- **Operational.** Signup now depends on mail delivery. A deployment whose mail is not configured can no longer create accounts.
