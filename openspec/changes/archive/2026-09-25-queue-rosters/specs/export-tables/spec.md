## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Export acts on no seat and no money
No Export tab SHALL offer an action on a row that changes who holds a seat or what anybody owes. Export states the tournament as it stands, for it to leave; promotion and return to the queue belong to the Queue phase, and every money action to Payments.

The rating correction is not such an action and SHALL remain: it changes what a roster is seeded by, not a seat or a sum.

#### Scenario: No arrows on Export
- **WHEN** the organizer opens a discipline tab on Export holding a queue
- **THEN** no row offers promotion or return to the queue, and the rating remains correctable
